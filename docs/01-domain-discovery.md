# 01 — Domain Discovery & Research Log

**Status:** OPEN

## Research approach

Prefer observing the actual restaurant workflow and asking the owner/staff over copying a competitor feature list. Record sources and dates for external claims. Separate observed facts, stakeholder statements, assumptions and AI suggestions.

## Interview checklist

### Daily operations
- How does a table open and close?
- Can one table have multiple rounds/orders?
- Can orders be merged, transferred, split or moved to another table?
- Who can edit an order, and until which stage?
- How do staff communicate with the kitchen today?
- Does each order item require modifiers, notes, add-ons or doneness options?
- How are unavailable items handled?
- Are takeaway/delivery orders needed?

### Payment and pricing
- Payment methods: cash, bank transfer, card, other?
- Is split payment required? Split by person, item or amount?
- Discounts, service charge, VAT/tax, rounding and receipt requirements?
- What happens when the wrong amount is entered or a payment is reversed?
- Is a fiscal/e-invoice system required? Verify relevant local law with authoritative sources/professional advice.

### Inventory and reporting
- Is stock tracking part of MVP? Recipe-level ingredient consumption or only item quantities?
- Stock units, recipes, waste, returns and stocktaking?
- What totals does the owner need each day/shift?

### Staff and permissions
- Accounts/PINs? Roles? Shift open/close?
- Who may void, discount, refund, edit menu/prices, see reports?
- Does every sensitive action need a reason and audit trail?

### Hardware and network
- Host PC OS/specs and account permissions?
- Receipt printer model, connection type, driver, paper width, cash drawer?
- Need kitchen printer or display?
- Can guest Wi-Fi reach the POS host? Is client isolation enabled?
- What should happen if Wi-Fi, power, PC or printer fails?
- Who performs updates and backup/restore?
